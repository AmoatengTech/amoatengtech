FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything parents usually ask before the first doodle.

Is ColourInBook really free?

Yes, ColourInBook is currently 100% free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-up and no credit card. Just open the page and play.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, all four activities keep working without an internet connection, so it is ideal for journeys and Wi-Fi-free afternoons.

What ages is it for?

Children aged 2 to 9. Toddler mode (2 to 4) offers six colours, extra-large buttons and picture labels; Kid mode (5 to 9) unlocks twelve colours, adjustable brush sizes and pinch-to-zoom.

Where does my child's artwork go?

Nowhere. ColourInBook collects no data and has no accounts. Work in progress is kept privately in your own browser, and pictures only leave the device when you tap "Save my picture" or "Print".

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes, ColourInBook is built mobile-first and adapts to phones, tablets, laptops and desktop screens, in portrait or landscape. All you need is a browser.

Ready to create something wonderful?

No download. No sign-up. Just tap and paint.
